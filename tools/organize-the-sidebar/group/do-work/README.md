## Why one builder (no per-op targets)

A membership change is fully described by the two graphs. The diff (`groupGraphWriteSet`) decides what to write; the name of the operation that produced it (add/move/group/ungroup or anything invented later) is irrelevant. Adding a new leaf operation needs ZERO change here.
