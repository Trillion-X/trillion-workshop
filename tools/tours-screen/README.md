# guided-overlay/

Guided tours over the workspace: one host, mounted once, runs any tour given to it as DATA. A tour is a list of steps (what to point at, the words, which side the card sits on) and triggers (which event starts it, a key that keeps it from showing twice, and tours it waits for). The person's own AIs write tours too (src/tours.mjs, MCP save_tour): the host runs the app's tours and the person's own (input `own-walkthroughs`) as one set, and starts one an AI asked to show now (input `walkthrough-show-asked`, once per ask). Brought from bitfield-packages `packages/guided-overlay` with only what runs: its engine, fold, host and surface; the person's progress is one record in their own Trillion store, written through the workspace's label-addressed records (`#workspace-records`, saved by `/work/api/records`), in place of the package's runtime log owners.

| `show/WalkthroughSurface.tsx` | Runs one tour: finds the step's target (retrying while the screen mounts, opening its screen first when the step says so), dims around it, draws the card beside it, and moves on. While its target is being found the step already shows its own words, centred with no spotlight, and moves onto the target once it is there (never a placeholder, never its words on the previous step's spotlight); when two matches show, the first fully in view is the target. The target's place is read again every frame while the step shows, so the ring and the card follow it when something above it opens or a pane slides in. The card is narrow (20rem): its progress sits on its own row above the words and its buttons on the row below; it sits beside its target, or, with no room beside it, where it covers the least of it. A step marked `centered_when_target_missing` stays centred in its own words when its target is absent; any other says the part is not available, with Try again and the step's own button to move past it. Pointed and centred cards are one shell (`AnchoredCoachmark`, `centered` when there is nothing to point at), with no chrome of its own. Every step has exactly one way to end the tour, drawn in one place from DATA (`exitControlOf`: `look.exit` of the step, else the tour, else the defaults' `exit`): a small close button on the card's top row beside the progress, or End tour first among its buttons; never both, never neither (test/walkthroughs.test.mjs counts them on every step). A step has one action button that does the step: a plain Skip is never drawn (a `skip_to` button is a choice and stays), the action button is never disabled while the step waits, and a step that only waits for the person's own press gets Do it for me (`words.do_it_label`), which runs the step's `act`, else presses its target, and moves on. A tour or step picks its card's paper by name (`look.paper`, rows in `guided-overlay-defaults.json` `papers`). Ending a tour shows the app's `words.closing_step` ("Tours are always here") once, then closes. Buttons the host adds (`extraButtons`, such as N more waiting) follow the step's own on every card and run their `perform`, then close the tour. A tour or step marked `escapable: false` ignores Escape and presses outside it. A step's `status_note` (`waiting`, `ready` with `{field}` filled from its readback, `ready_without`) shows under its content as a tone dot and a few words while its condition waits and once it holds; the step stays. A targetless step's width is its `look.card_width_px`. A step with a target leaves a hole in the dimming over it, so the target takes presses and keys; once the target's own click has run, the tour moves on from that step if its `advance_condition` holds counting the target as used (no condition counts as holding). A step that opens its screen (`target_preparation`) asks for it again every `targeting.reopen_every_ms` (guided-overlay-defaults.json) while its target stays hidden, so a screen opened after it (the page's own address, a press) cannot leave the step waiting centred behind another screen. |

## Tours that play themselves

A definition's `play` (`{ step_ms, after_ms }`) makes the tour move by itself (`WalkthroughSurface`): each step shows for its own `look.hold_ms` (else `step_ms`), then its action button is pressed for the person, running the step's `act`, and what the act did stays on screen for `after_ms` (else `act.pause_ms`) before the next step; the last step waits for them. The person's own press or key outside the card (a trusted event) hands the page to them and the playing stops. A step's `look.spotlight` lays its `scrim_color` and `blur_px` over the tour's, so each step chooses whether the page dims and blurs; a scrim with no colour and no blur is `pass-all` (parts/ui/show/overlay-spotlight) and catches no click. Used by Trillion's live showcases (src/showcases.mjs).
