# computer/

What runs on a person's own computer to reach what only that computer can: Mac Contacts, iMessage in Messages, WhatsApp as a linked device, and the places read by themselves (Notes, Voice Memos, Messages, Calendar, Reminders, Mail, Photos, screenshots, Downloads). These are connections, the same job as a Gmail connection but for what lives on the computer; they wrap no AI. Nothing here is part of the hosted service at app.gettrillion.ai; the files a person installs are served at `<origin>/computer/<file>` (`src/downloads.mjs`).

**Depends on:** `@whiskeysockets/baileys` and `@noble/hashes` (this folder's `package.json`), macOS `osascript`, `sips`, `diskutil` and `node:sqlite`, `tools/browse-and-upload-my-files/do-work` (the shared sender and folder walker) and `file-type.mjs` and `side-files.mjs` for `keep-drive-files.mjs`, `tools/content-identity` for every BLAKE3 identity, a hearing row's own program (`mlx_whisper` for `hear-with-mlx-whisper.py`; `mlx_audio`, `mlx_whisper` and `constriction` for `hear-with-bark.py`; a row whose modules are missing is passed over with its `missing` words), and the hosted routes below.
