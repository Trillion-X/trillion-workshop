# parts/ui/show/composer

The one box for sending anything at once: words (with `@` mentions of the person's people, AIs, workflows, automations and actions, drawn as coloured chips), files (chosen, pasted or dropped), links, a voice recording, a camera video, and a screen recording, all waiting in a tray above the words until Send hands the whole message over in one piece. It is drawn where its caller puts it (a screen, or the message box screen the screen arrangement floats over the work in a layer, `src/workspace/surfaces.json` `trillion.message-box`). It never places itself over the page or in a window of its own.

## Behaviour

- Paste: files pasted become tray items; a paste that is only a link becomes a link card (`link-preview`), shown as its address and site until `readLink` answers with the card the page describes itself with; any other text pastes as words.
- Drop: files dropped anywhere on the box are added (react-dropzone, no click-to-open; the paperclip opens the chooser).
- Recording: the one recorder (`parts/record-audio/show/record-in-the-browser.ts`, the same one the Record screen uses; its sources, recording kinds and problem words are `parts/record-audio/read-data/capture-sources.json`). One at a time, shown live in the shared `recording-bar` (its time, the sound over time in `live-waveform`, or the picture itself), with Stop and add and Throw away; Pause and Resume hold it and its elapsed time leaves the pauses out. Sending while recording stops it first and sends it. A recording that cannot start, or a microphone that hears nothing, says so under the box in plain words (what happened and what to do); a recording the browser stops by itself (a device unplugged, a screen share ended from its bar) joins the tray with what it made.
- Words: a block editor (`parts/documents`). `# `, `- `, `1. `, `> `, `[] ` and the other shortcut rows make headings, lists, quotes and to-dos; Enter in a list continues it and Enter in an empty item ends it; `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~struck~~` and `` `code` `` are drawn once the caret leaves them; arrows move between blocks; Backspace at a block's start joins it upward. Enter elsewhere sends; Shift+Enter is a new line. While empty it shows the placeholder (the caller's `words.placeholder`), wrapped in whole words.
- Mentions: typing `@` (or the @ button) opens the picker over the caller's candidates; the chosen one becomes a chip in its kind's colour.
- Layout: as a chat input is laid out (21st.dev Input Bar 12400, Prompt Input 28187): the words fill the box, and one row under them holds the ways to add, bare (`ExpandableTabs` `frame="none"`) and flush with the words' start, with Send at the row's end. The box is a size container: below 18rem the ways to add wrap onto a second row rather than push Send out of the box (a container query, so a narrow desktop pane behaves as a phone does).
- Placement: the box has no floating, docked or corner mode of its own. Floating over the work is the screen arrangement's overlay layer, which moves, resizes, closes and docks it as a tab like any other window (the message box screen). The dock-to-an-edge, floating-panel, corner-button and keep-on-top (Document Picture-in-Picture) modes were deleted on 2026-10-01.
- A box pinned at the bottom of its screen lifts clear of a control floating over the window's bottom edge (`../floating-occlusion/lift-above-floating`).
- Draw opens the screen `composer-words.json` `draw.screen` names (the board, `trillion.board`) by the placeable-surface open the sidebar uses, titled `draw.title`, opened with `draw.input` (`{ "tool": "draw" }`, drawing already chosen), in the shared open choice `draw.opens_as` names (`open-as-window`: its own floating window, so it never splits the message box's window). Where the words name no screen, Draw is not offered. The box has no drawing space of its own (DrawPad was deleted 2026-10-02).
- While a message is being handled the shared border beam (`../border-beam`) carries the thread round the box's border, beside the busy line under it.
- Words the send answered show under the box as a status line; a failure shows as an alert and keeps everything for another try. The same unchanged draft object is reused after a failure (`retry-draft.ts`) so the sender can retain its retry identity; changing its text, files, links or mentions, or sending after success, starts a new draft.
