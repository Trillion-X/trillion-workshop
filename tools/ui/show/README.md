# ui/show/

Browser-local visual controls that packages may import directly when they want
a source dependency instead of runtime address/reply material.

The Coss record editor supports DATA-defined numeric and read-only fields and
asynchronous row actions. Completed actions merge only returned fields into the
current surviving row; they cannot restore a removed row or overwrite unrelated
edits. Imperative Coss dialogs use source cancellation/propagation to dismiss only
the top dialog across independently mounted consumers.

These files must not read feeds, invoke targets, import feature packages, or
own durable work.

If behavior needs durable data, address reads, or target invokes, it belongs in
the package that owns that behavior, not under `packages/ui/`.

## Current Pure Parts

- `asked-now/`: `useAskedNow(value, storageKey, act, ready?, freshMs?)` acts once on an "asked now" record (`{ asked_at, ... }`) the person's AI writes through their store (start a tour, open a layout): once per ask per browser, never for an ask already old when the page opened.
- `ai-app-bridge/`: the one MCP Apps bridge every view an AI app (ChatGPT, Claude) draws speaks: `connectToAiApp(win, { appName, onToolResult, onToolInput, content })` answers `ui/initialize`, hears each tool result once (also from `window.openai`), hands on the call's arguments while the AI writes them (`ui/notifications/tool-input-partial`, `partial: true`) and then whole once (`ui/notifications/tool-input` or `window.openai.toolInput`), and gives `callTool(name, args)` (tools/call, or `window.openai.callTool`), `reportSize()`, `placement()` (the display mode and the most height the app gives, kept from `ui/initialize`, `ui/notifications/host-context-changed` or `window.openai`), `onPlacement(listener)`, `askDisplayMode(mode)` (`ui/request-display-mode` or `window.openai.requestDisplayMode`; answers the mode given) and `openLink(url)`; `aiAppViewDocument({ hostId, wordsId, words, scriptUrl, stylesheetUrl, module, baseUrl })` is the view's HTML document (a host, its words as JSON, its script and stylesheet).
- `file-view-document/`: the HTML document an AI app (ChatGPT, Claude) loads to show one file a tool pointed at (MCP Apps `text/html;profile=mcp-app`): a host, the copy as JSON and the app's script and stylesheet (`fileViewDocument({ words, scriptUrl, stylesheetUrl })`, an `aiAppViewDocument`); `file-view-bridge.mjs` hears the tool result's `url`, `media_type` and `name` over the MCP Apps bridge or `window.openai.toolOutput` through `ai-app-bridge` (`listenForShownFile(win, show)`).
- `region-map/`: `RegionMap`, one tappable drawing of any set of named regions (vector paths as DATA), coloured in five heat steps by a number per region and picked by pointer or keyboard; `regionValuesFromRecords` reads records into those numbers from a placement row (region field, value field, a chart formula), `regionHeatSteps` is the pure step maths. A region with no reading is empty, never zero. Appearance: `parts/screen-look/show/region-map.css`.
- `chart/`: the one chart family (`Chart`, `ChartPlacement`, `ChartPlacements`, `ChartsOver`): line, area, bar, stacked bars, sparkline, ring and heat map drawn from ChartData, every chart scrubbed by pointer, touch or arrow keys with a tooltip card, budget and target lines labelled where they start, beautiful empty, loading and one-reading states, a narrow layout and motion; a chart placement DATA row (records, number, grouping, formula, time bucket, lines) makes the data, rolls up and drills down by bucket, and is offered as a Chart view on every list. Marks, formulas, buckets and value readers are registered maps. Look in `screen-look/show/chart.css`.
- `audio-timeline/`: a recording's sound as a timeline (`AudioTimeline`): the place listened to stays under a fixed middle line while the sound slides beneath it, drag scrubs, pinch zooms around the line, and a chosen part sits between two slider handles; `peaksOf` and `timelineTime` beside it.
- `composer/`: the one box for sending anything at once (`Composer`): words with `@` mentions drawn as coloured chips (`mention-field/`), chosen, pasted or dropped files, links, voice, camera and screen recordings wait in a tray until Send hands them over in one piece, and Draw opens the board; floating over the work is the screen arrangement's layer (the message box screen), never a window of its own. What can be recorded and every word are DATA beside it.
- `mention-field/`: the words part of every message box (`MentionField`): plain writing with spelling help where `@` offers the caller's candidates in the shared `mention-picker/` and drops each chosen one in as a coloured chip; the token model and key handling are the documents part's.
- `mention-picker/`: what `@` shows anywhere a person writes (`MentionPicker`): things of every kind grouped under their kind with its icon in its colour, narrowed by the typed words, and a row of kinds that narrows to one kind or widens back to all (`pickMentions`, `mentionKindsAmong`).
- `record-chip/`: the one live chip for a mentioned thing of any kind (`RecordChip`): its kind's icon in its colour, then its name; pressing it opens the thing. `RecordChipProvider` hands down the kinds' looks and where things open; `MentionWords` draws words with their mentions as chips (message bubbles, space posts); `mentionRuns` finds a kept message's mentions in its words.
- `fact-chips/`: `FactChips`, small chips chosen and ordered by field DATA that say what a thing is at a glance and open on hover into its details and actions; ported unchanged from the Bitfield ui package, its own test run in a real browser by `test/fact-chips-package-test.test.mjs`.
- `listing-card/`: the one glance shape (`ListingCard`, `ListingGrid`): a thing's icon, face or logo leads, its name is the title, when sits muted under it, the one key value stands alone on the right with its unit small, the rest are icon-led facts and chips with faces, and state is a tinted tile or a dot; a `card` or a one-line `row` form from DATA. Home's list rows, the Workshop, the Marketplace and Spaces draw through it.
- `stage-inset/`: the one owner of the room docked chrome keeps at the window's edges (`registerStageInset`, `updateStageInset`, `unregisterStageInset`), shared by the sidebar and the docked message box.
- `picture-window/`: deleted 2026-10-01. Its only caller was the composer's keep-on-top window; a window over the work is a screen-arrangement layer.
- `file-field/`: one form field that takes a file the person keeps (`FileField`): chosen from their files in the shared folder tree (only files of the kind it takes, `file-takes.json` DATA), or dropped or uploaded through the licensed Dropzone; it holds the file's id and shows the chosen file as the shared file chip. The guide draws it for a row field with `kind: 'file'`.

Reference anatomy: 21st.dev 28770 and 29473, Linear My issues, Asana My tasks.
- `display-mark/`: `DisplayMark`, the one identity mark (a picture, a glyph, or a person's initials from `initialsOf`, the only place letters are made for a mark); shape `person` or `tile` with radii from the look; tint, corner badge and member stacks. `person-tag/`, coss profile, people, rank and thread parts, `linked-identity/`, `live-surface-people/` and workspace projects all draw through it; a thing is given a glyph, never letters.
- `person-tag/`: `PersonTag`, the assignee chip: the person's `DisplayMark` with an optional name.
- `run-card/`: one work run (title and what it was for, humanized status chip, time, outputs, safe error) composed from status-vocabulary, output-field-list, and safe-error-card; never an avatar.
- `safe-error-card/`: failure card whose `sanitizeErrorText` gate strips stack frames, paths, addresses, hashes, and JSON dumps before any copy renders.
- `status-vocabulary/`: pure raw-state -> { label, tone, isTerminal, isError } translation with package override maps; unknown states humanize instead of leaking.
- `timeseries-chart/`: local chart rendering over caller-owned buckets, summary, and callbacks.
- `tilt/`: MIT source-first port of Motion Primitives' pointer-normalized spring tilt, adapted to the installed `framer-motion` owner with reduced-motion and coarse-pointer denial.
- `toggle/`: controlled boolean switch; caller owns state and persistence.
- `virtual-list/`: threshold-gated windowed list over `@tanstack/react-virtual`; renders only the visible slice of arbitrarily large item arrays (spacer + translated absolute rows) so consuming surfaces stay O(visible) DOM at any N; caller owns items, keys, and row rendering.
- `work-start-state/`: compact Studio starter/empty-state card with optional
  step tiles, action slot, and matching skeleton rows. Callers own labels,
  actions, and package meaning.

## Compatibility Boundary

Copied package surfaces may pass older visual aliases such as `tone`, `muted`,
`solid`, `flex="1"`, arbitrary numeric `gridCols`, nullable icon names, and
CSS-string progress heights. The UI primitives normalize those aliases here so
feature packages stay dumb renderers and do not grow local style adapters.
# Source color chooser

`balsa/color-picker.tsx` exports `mountColorPicker(node, {id, label, value, disabled, onChange})`, returning `getValue()` for imperative forms. Uses upstream MIT Balsa ColorPicker and its full original dependency closure. Values normalize to six-digit hex; unsaved form changes remain local. Existing shared root cleanup unmounts picker listeners when the host is removed. Shared appearance lives in `screen-look/show/balsa`, including scoped source tokens and contained panel layout. Exact source identities are in `balsa/provenance.json`.

`coss/workspace-controls.tsx` record forms support boolean fields through the
original Coss Checkbox. `allowAdd` and `allowRemove` default to true; fixed-record
forms can disable those controls without rebuilding row markup. Expansion's exact
range rough-cut form uses these shared fields and opens the protected media player
for preview. Other record forms retain add/remove behavior.

`coss/workspace-controls.tsx` also exports `mountEmpty(node, {title?, description?, action?, onAction?, tone?, icon?})`
and `mountMediaLoading(node, {label?, layout?: 'gallery'|'rows'|'preview'|'editor'})` for hosts that draw by call
(the Mac app's plain scripts). Both hand their state to `screen-state` (`<ScreenState>`), so they draw nothing of
their own: `tone:'error'` with an action becomes the failed state with that action as its retry; without one it is an
empty state carrying the alert icon. Workspace screens never call them; they read through `loadScreen`/`useScreenLoad`.
Provider logos everywhere in this file come from `provider-mark/shipped-provider-icons.ts`.
