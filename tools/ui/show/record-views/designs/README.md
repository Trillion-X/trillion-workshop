# record-views/designs

The designs a list's view can be drawn in, and the designed views every list is offered: a chat, a feed, the guide's sectioned rows, swipe cards, a calendar, a two-field matrix, sprints, one document, a turning carousel and Draw (the one canvas, row id `canvas`: the list on the real board, to draw, write and put shapes around) are DATA rows of `designed-views.json`, each naming a design this folder registers (Draw's design is registered by the workspace, `src/workspace/offer-draw-design.tsx`, because the board is a workspace screen).

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `designed-views.json` | DATA: the designed views every list is offered (`id`, `label`, `icon`, `design`, `needs` slots, `options`). Adding a view is one row. |
| `chat-design.tsx` | `ChatDesign`: records as one conversation on the shared `../../chat` part (slots `body`, `title`, `author`, `when`): oldest at the top, newest at the bottom with the list's `compose` pinned under it; a record the list's `nesting` holds under another is a message answering it, drawn in its time with what it answers quoted; a message's Reply puts what it answers over the box and the next message goes under it (`compose.box(reply)` or `compose.send({text, parent})`). The list's extras (who wrote it and whether it was the reader, a shared thing, reactions, its menu, a quiet line for words taken back, `unseen`) are read in the thread design's shape. |
| `offer-designed-views.tsx` | Registers the `chat`, `thread`, `rows`, `inbox`, `swipe`, `calendar`, `matrix`, `sprints`, `document` and `carousel` designs (each fetched the first time a list draws it) and offers every row of `designed-views.json` to every list whose fields fill it. Imported once by `src/workspace/main.tsx`. |
| `thread-design.tsx` | `ThreadDesign`: records as posts of the shared `../../coss/thread` (slots `body`, `title`, `author`, `when`); replies nest under the record the list's `nesting` holds them under (the group graph the Tree view reads too, `nestedByIdentity`), to any depth; the list's `compose` is the box (on top or under, from the row's `options.box`) and writes every reply under its post. |
| `rows-design.tsx` | `RowsDesign`: records as the shared guide's rows (slots `title`, `detail`, `status`, `section`), sectioned by the person's Group by (else the `section` slot), each record's lead row actions as its buttons and Open when the row's `options.open` names it. The Tours screen opens in it. |
| `inbox-design.tsx` | `InboxDesign`: a shared inbox. Each record no other record holds is a conversation (slots `title`, `body`, `author`, `when`, `status`, `person`), listed newest activity first beside the open one, whose messages read oldest first with the list's compose under them; the open conversation's lead row actions are its buttons (Take it, Done). Below `narrow_below_px` of its own width one pane shows at a time. Spaces offer it as the conversation's Inbox view. |
| `swipe-design.tsx` | `SwipeDesign`: records as a swipeable stack of work cards drawn by `../../swipe-deck/SwipeDeck` (the next cards show under the top one; the dragged card tilts and shows its side in the colour of the row's `left_tone` / `right_tone`) (slots `title`, `detail`, `status`, `tour`); a swipe sets the next or earlier declared status, with undo. |
| `design-parts.tsx` | The pieces several designs draw with: `DesignCard` (a record's picture, title opening it, status badge and words; `size` small or large) and `PlacedCells` (columns across, rows down the side or headed above, each cell holding the records `placeOf` puts there; a card dragged, or moved with the arrow keys, to another cell is reported through `onPlace`, the cell it is over drawn ringed and said aloud). |
| `calendar-design.tsx` | `CalendarDesign`: a list with a date on the shared `../../schedule-calendar` (month board and hour lanes, its header choosing among the row's `options.views`); records sit on their day (slot `when`), coloured by `status`, with no date in the tray; pressing opens, a day's number shows that day (`options.day_view`), and a drag to a day, an hour or the tray writes the date (ISO time, or null) when the list keeps changes. |
| `matrix-design.tsx` | `MatrixDesign`: a list as a grid of two fields of declared values (slots `across` and `down`, by default the first two that are not the status), cards in the cell of their two values, records missing one in a column or row of their own (`options.unset`); a move writes the values that change. The idea of `parts/group/show/arrange-matrix.ts`, as cells that grow with what they hold. |
| `sprints-design.tsx` | `SprintsDesign`: a list with a date and a status (slots `when`, `status`) as periods of `options.period_days` days from the week start (`options.week_starts_on`) of the earliest record, each a row of status columns headed by its dates, how many are done (`views-offered-to-every-list.json` `done`, through `doneValuesOf`) and a bar; only periods holding records and the current one are drawn, records with no date last (`options.undated`). A move to another column writes the status; to another period, moves the date by whole periods. |
| `document-design.tsx` | `DocumentDesign`: the whole list as one document, each record a numbered part (slot `title` the heading, `body` else `detail` the paragraph, then as many other facts as the zoom step shows), sectioned by the person's Group by; where the list keeps changes the heading and paragraph are written in place (shared `InlineEditText`) and each change reaches the list; `options.open` names each part's Open. Replaces the record views' own read-only Document view. |
| `carousel-design.tsx` | `CarouselDesign`: the list as a ring of large cards on the shared `../../cult/rotating-carousel` (picture, title, status, words); arrows, swipe and arrow keys turn it, the card in front opens on press, with Open (`options.open`), its lead row actions and its menu under it. |
| `records-on-a-board.ts` | `recordsOnABoard(context, row)`: a list as a board source (`{cards, lines}`, the shape `parts/group/do-work/board-source.ts` draws), read by the Draw design (`src/workspace/draw-design.tsx`): each card its `title` words, its state (the `status` slot, else the list's `statusField`, else the first field whose values carry a tone: label, the declared value's tone, a pulse while the value is a working one), the row actions that apply to it, its frame the `group` slot or the first Group by; lines from the list's `links` (with their words and kind), from fields declaring `links` (`../view-designs` `recordLinks`) and from the list's nesting, each pair once per kind. |
| `README.md` | This file. |

## Key Types / Functions

| Name | Shape | Role |
|------|-------|------|
| `ChatDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The chat design. Options: `empty`, `reply`, `words` (the chat part's `ChatThreadWords`, plus `yourself` for a reply to the reader's own message). |
| `ThreadDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The thread design. Options: `style` (`cards` or `discussion`), `order` (`newest-first`/`newest-last`), `box` (`top`/`bottom`/`none`), `empty`, `reply`. |
| `RowsDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The rows design. Options: `open` (the words of each row's Open button), `empty`. |
| `CalendarDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The calendar design. Options: `views` (`[{id, label, view: {grid: 'month'} \| {grid: 'hours', days}}]`), `day_view`, `words` (`previous`, `next`, `today`, `show`, `tray`, `tray_empty`, `more`, `placing`, `unplacing`; `{count}`, `{title}`, `{when}` filled in), `snap_minutes`, `item_minutes`, `start_scroll_minute`. |
| `MatrixDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The matrix design. Options: `unset`, `moving` (`{title}`, `{across}`, `{down}`), `empty`. |
| `SprintsDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The sprints design. Options: `period_days`, `week_starts_on` (0 Sunday), `undated`, `unset`, `done` (`{done}`, `{count}`), `moving` (`{title}`, `{status}`), `empty`. |
| `DocumentDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The document design. Options: `open`, `untitled`, `write_here`, `empty`. |
| `CarouselDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The carousel design. Options: `open`, `empty`. |
| `recordsOnABoard` | `(context: RecordViewContext, row: DesignedViewRow) => {cards: BoardCard[], lines: BoardLine[]}` | `BoardCard` `{id, text, state?: {label, tone?, pulse?}, actions?: {id, label}[], group?}`, `BoardLine` `{from, to, label?, kind?}`. |
| `SwipeDesign` | `({context, row}: ViewDesignProps) => JSX` | The swipe design. Options: `left_label`, `right_label`, `empty`, `label`. |
| `DesignCard` | `({context, slots, record, size?} & HTMLAttributes) => JSX` | One record's card; extra attributes (drag handlers) land on the card. |
| `PlacedCells` | `({label, rows: PlacedRow[], columns: PlacedColumn[], records, identify, placeOf, renderCard, onPlace?, corner?, moving?}) => JSX` | Cells records are placed in; `PlacedRow` `{key, label?, header?, current?}`, `PlacedColumn` `{key, label}`, `CellPlace` `{row, column}`. |

## Invariants

- No list, field or product word is named here: slots come from the row or the fields' `role`, words from the list.
- A row naming a design nobody registered is not offered; a row is offered only to a list whose fields fill its `needs`.
