# tools/send-to-trillion-from-my-browser/

Send to Trillion: a browser extension (Chrome, Arc, Dia, Edge, Brave; any Chromium browser) that sends what the person finds on any page (the page, a selection, a picture of what they see, the whole page's text and links, a video of the page or a recording of the tab, and anything added by right-clicking on other pages) to their Trillion, with a saved instruction or their own words, where their connected AIs take it and act.


## Where it lives

This folder is the whole extension: it moved here from the app repository's `extension/` folder, so a path written `extension/...` below is this folder (`src/popup.tsx`, `words.json`, `manifest.json`), and `tools/ui`, `tools/addressed-marks` and the other shared parts are the Workshop tools beside it (`tools/ui`, `tools/addressed-marks`, `tools/feel`, `tools/screen-look`). `moved-from.json` names every file's source. The build needs the packages the shared parts import (react, lucide-react, tailwindcss, @tailwindcss/node and the rest); they are the Workshop's own dependencies in its root `package.json`, so `npm install` at the Workshop root is all the build needs.

```
EXTENSION_OUT=<folder> node tools/send-to-trillion-from-my-browser/scripts/build-extension.mjs
```

`scripts/build-extension.mjs` writes the folder a Chromium browser loads unpacked (`scripts/named-icons.mjs` gives the bundle only the icons the extension and the shared controls name; `scripts/resolve-moved-files.mjs` answers a shared part's import of a web-client file from the receipts of the tool that now holds it). `part.json` makes the Mac app's placer entry (`offer-in-browsers`) loadable as `workshop:send-to-trillion-from-my-browser/offer-in-browsers`. Tests: `node --test tools/send-to-trillion-from-my-browser/test/*.test.mjs`. The store listing `store-listing.json` is written by the app repository's `scripts/how-to-start-for-reviewers.mjs` from this folder's `manifest.json`.

## A free account

When a free account asks for something only a paid plan does (a page tool, saving a chat, keeping a file), the service's refusal carries `upgrade_needed` and the feature (MCP `_meta`, `src/tools.mjs`), and the popup shows what a paid plan unlocks with the free and paid plans side by side instead of an error. **Choose this plan** opens the offer's Buy on Trillion, `/buy/<offer id>?from=extension&return=<the page they were on>`; after paying, Trillion's `/welcome` shows the steps back to that page. Buy pays for the account signed in on Trillion in this browser (and makes a new account when nobody is), so while this browser is signed out there the card says to sign in first and Choose opens the sign-in.

## Where a send goes

A send is one record of the person's own. By default it is a `shared` record and Trillion files it; one tap on File in picks one of the person's lists instead, and the send becomes a row of that list. The lists are the person's own `list` records, read each time the popup opens; a new list shows the next time, nothing is rebuilt. The iPhone Share sheet writes the same record.

## Add to it

Under the page, the popup offers four capture buttons. Each lands in their Files and shows in Sending with what it is and its size (a row saying what it is doing while it works), so the next Send carries it:

- **Record audio**: just the person's voice, no screen, like the iPhone share sheet. The first time, the extension's own microphone page asks (a popup cannot show the browser's question) and starts the recording once allowed; afterwards it starts from the popup. While it records, the popup shows the live waveform, the time and **Stop and keep**; it is kept as `/Sent to Trillion/<date time> Voice note.webm`.

- **Record this tab**: records what the person does in the tab, with its sound, until they press **Stop and keep** (or close the tab). The toolbar button shows REC while it records; the popup can be closed. The video is kept as `/Sent to Trillion/<date time> <title>.mp4` (WebM on a browser that cannot make MP4).
- **Add the whole page**: Trillion reads the page on its own compute with the Workshop tool `tool:read-public-web-page` (public pages only: private addresses are refused, 2 MB at most, 30,000 characters and 100 links) and keeps its title, text and links as `/Sent to Trillion/<date time> <title>.json`.
- **Make a video of this page**: Trillion opens the page in a browser in the cloud with `tool:record-a-web-demo`, scrolls through it (the steps are the row's DATA), and keeps `demo.mp4`, `demo.gif` and a cover picture under `/Made by tools/Record a demo of a web page/<when>/`. It takes a few minutes, so the popup says so and offers Open Files.

The two Workshop rows run as the signed-in person through `@tools` `tools:run`, the same road any AI on the account takes; a page behind a sign-in cannot be read or filmed that way, and Record this tab is the way to capture it.

## Trillion's notices

What Trillion has for the person reaches this browser as a notification with buttons that do the next thing. When the extension starts, and right after the person signs in, it gets the public VAPID application key through `tool:my-browser-push-application-key`, subscribes to push, and registers that subscription through `tool:register-my-notification-device` as a Workshop `web-push` device. Reads and actions go through the same signed-in account and the registered opaque device ID.

- **A notice arrives**: it shows with its title and words, and its first two actions as buttons (Chrome shows at most two). A `time-sensitive` notice stays until it is dismissed; how loud each level is lives in `settings.json` `notices.levels`.
- **A button**: sends the exact action id through `workflow:act-on-my-notice` with a stable submission ID; the account decides what it does and answers what it did, shown as a short notification, and opens Trillion where it says. The extension never knows what an action means.
- **An action that asks for words** (Reply): a notification has no text field, so it opens the reply window with the notice and a box; Enter (or Send) posts the words with the action's id, Shift+Enter is a new line, Escape closes it.
- **The notice itself**: opens Trillion where the notice points, in the Trillion tab already open, and marks it read for this browser's device scope.
- **The badge**: the number of unread notices in the worn look's accent, counted when a notice arrives, at start and every five minutes. While a recording runs the badge says REC; with nothing unread it shows the tray's count.

## Build and install

```
node tools/send-to-trillion-from-my-browser/scripts/build-extension.mjs          # writes dist/extension/
```

Each page carries only the files it uses: the build runs the workspace's own bundle plugins (`scripts/barrel-imports.mjs` reads a part's index as the files it names, `scripts/part-side-effects.mjs` marks a part's files as doing nothing when imported, `scripts/named-icons.mjs` keeps only the icons named), then refuses any page over `budgets.json`. When a page grows past it, read the build's metafile for the page and cut the import it never calls (as 1.3.1 did: the person's own sound looks moved to `tools/ui/show/sound-signal/own-sound-looks.ts`, and `src/usage-meters.mjs` reads the cloud-drive records only when a price is asked for).

The stylesheet opens with the look's button tokens (`buttonLookCss` from `scripts/make-device-looks.mjs`, for the first-launch look `settings.json` names; each page then wears the look the person wears in Trillion, `src/worn-look-page.ts`; host `chrome-extension` in `tools/screen-look/device-hosts.json`, whose `look_literals` refuse a colour or face decided in `src/`): the buttons are the shared ui Button at the look's control corner, never pills, with the web's light top edge and press.

Open it with its toolbar button, its keyboard shortcut (Option-Shift-S), or right-click anything and choose **Add to Trillion**.

To update after a change: pull, run the build again, then press the reload arrow on the Send to Trillion card on the extensions page (the folder stays the same, so the pin and sign-in are kept).

To try a build before it is on the store: open the browser's extensions page (`chrome://extensions`, `arc://extensions` or `dia://extensions`), turn on Developer mode, choose **Load unpacked**, pick `dev/trillion-apps/dist/extension`, pin **Send to Trillion**, click it and sign in.

Proof against the live service, in a real Chromium (builds a proof copy that is lent pages as a host permission, since a script cannot click the toolbar button that lends a person's page):

```
EXTENSION_OUT=dist/extension-proof EXTENSION_PAGE_ACCESS='<all_urls>' node tools/send-to-trillion-from-my-browser/scripts/build-extension.mjs
SHOW_BROWSER=1 node scripts/prove-send-to-trillion.mjs <profile folder> <page url> <screenshots folder>
ALLOWLIST=1 node scripts/prove-more-from-this-page.mjs <profile folder> <page url> <screenshots folder> [before]
```

`prove-more-from-this-page.mjs` makes a proof account (free, then paid with the proof coupon, ended and deleted at the end) and proves the plan cards and the paid card's Buy, the whole page row with its size, the popup kept as left after closing, Record audio with its waveform, and Record this tab. The browser's `--use-fake-ui-for-media-stream` (which answers the microphone question for a script) makes tab capture fail with "Requested device not found", so the tab is recorded after starting the browser again without it; `ALLOWLIST=1` adds `--allowlisted-extension-id`, since a script cannot click the toolbar button that lets a person's click grant a tab's capture.

Listed on the Chrome Web Store as [Send to Trillion](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/send-to-trillion/hlcppfhpcfmlinjmjkiadmbnnboadlnh) (item `hlcppfhpcfmlinjmjkiadmbnnboadlnh`, publisher Trillion); `src/devices.json` offers that link everywhere Trillion offers its apps. A new version goes out by raising `version` in `manifest.json`, building with `scripts/build-extension.mjs`, zipping the built folder and uploading it as the item's new package in the developer dashboard, which reviews it before installed copies update.
