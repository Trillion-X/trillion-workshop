## Feature anatomy from official documentation

- **Calendar and connected accounts:** Google Calendar two-way sync; multiple Google accounts; select which calendars appear; set colors and a default calendar; sync events, attendees, reminders, recurrence and conferencing links. Apple Calendar is listed for desktop. Account settings describe OAuth, reconnect/disconnect, and calendar removal behavior. [Google Calendar integration](https://amie.so/documentation/google-calendar-integration), [Account settings](https://amie.so/documentation/account/settings), [Quick Start](https://amie.so/documentation/getting-started/quick-start)
- **Calendar surface and navigation:** a unified calendar with tasks alongside events; a desktop menu-bar calendar for upcoming events, details and one-click call join. Documented shortcuts include a global quick menu, Calendar/Inbox switching, full view, and joining the next call. [Quick Start](https://amie.so/documentation/getting-started/quick-start), [Menu Bar Calendar](https://amie.so/documentation/desktop/menubar)
- **Tasks and planning:** natural-language entry, quick capture, inbox for unscheduled tasks, calendar time blocks, drag/drop scheduling and duration resize. Task fields include due time, deadline, priority, labels, duration, recurrence and integrations. Lists/projects can have colors, preferred scheduling times and archive behavior. [Tasks & Todos](https://amie.so/documentation/features/tasks)
- **AI scheduling:** place unscheduled tasks into free calendar time using duration, deadline, priority, work hours, list preferences, buffer time and calendar visibility; allow manual drag/drop changes. External scheduling links share availability and book a selected slot. [AI Scheduling](https://amie.so/documentation/features/ai-scheduling), [Scheduling Links](https://amie.so/documentation/scheduling-links)
- **Meetings and recordings:** the desktop app records local system audio without adding a meeting bot, then uploads for transcription, summaries and extracted action items. Docs describe speaker attribution, editable transcripts/summaries/tasks, and turning action items into todos. [AI Meeting Notes](https://amie.so/documentation/features/ai-notes), [Google Meet](https://amie.so/documentation/google-meet-integration), [Zoom](https://amie.so/documentation/zoom-integration)
- **Notes and follow-through:** event-linked meeting pages combine transcript, summary, decisions, attendees and action items. Pages can be searched, edited, shared to Slack/CRM or by link, and exported. [Pages](https://amie.so/documentation/features/pages), [Slack integration](https://amie.so/documentation/slack-integration)
- **Email:** the documented email feature learns from sent-message style and drafts follow-ups using meeting or collection context; the user reviews and copies the draft to an email client. These docs do not establish a full inbox, send, archive or mailbox-management experience. [Email Integration](https://amie.so/documentation/email-integration)
- **Other connections:** Google account supports calendar, Meet and contacts; Zoom supports meeting links/joining; Slack shares meeting notes; Attio, HubSpot, Pipedrive and Salesforce appear in CRM context and note-sharing workflows. [Account settings](https://amie.so/documentation/account/settings), [Google Meet](https://amie.so/documentation/google-meet-integration), [Zoom](https://amie.so/documentation/zoom-integration), [Pages](https://amie.so/documentation/features/pages)
- **Settings and account management:** profile, timezone, light/dark/system appearance, date and clock format, week start, weekend visibility, reminders and email preferences, connected accounts, working hours, calendar visibility/defaults, scheduling exclusions, transcription language, data export/delete, and billing/team plans. Feature availability can vary by plan. [Account settings](https://amie.so/documentation/account/settings), [Billing & Plans](https://amie.so/documentation/account/billing)
